Who We Are

A transnational vision — spiritually, economically, intellectually

Founded by Anuraag Hooda, a Columbia University student who studied abroad in Rabat, Tangier, and Fez (Summer 2025), IndoRoccan connects founders, researchers, and institutions across India and Morocco. We believe the next wave of innovation will emerge from unexpected geographies — and this corridor is one of the most underexplored in the world.

Mission

To foster bold collaboration across education, innovation, AI, and entrepreneurship between South Asia and North Africa, starting with India and Morocco.

What We Do

Six pillars of cross-regional innovation

From early-stage incubation to AI-powered intelligence, IndoRoccan provides the infrastructure for transnational ventures to take root and scale.

🌱

Startup Incubation

Incubate early ideas and cross-regional startups at the intersection of Indian and Moroccan markets, from idea through MVP.

🌍

Diaspora Ventures

Support diaspora-led ventures and student innovators who have roots in both ecosystems or seek to bridge them.

🎓

Academic Exchanges

Explore grant-funded academic and cultural exchanges connecting universities across South Asia and North Africa.

🤝

Institutional Connections

Connect founders, researchers, and institutions across continents — including embassies, IITs, and Moroccan universities.

🤖

AI Intelligence Engine

Leverage AI to track India-Morocco signals across diplomacy, defense, trade, and startups in 10 languages.

💰

Funding Access

Connect cross-border founders with pre-seed and seed capital, grant opportunities, and impact investors who care about emerging corridors.
